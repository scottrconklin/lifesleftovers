Questions

How does pickup work?

After Closing Night you pick a time the seller offered. The exact address is shared once you’ve paid.

What if it isn’t as described?

Don’t confirm pickup. Report it and you’re refunded before the seller is paid.

What can’t I sell?

Weapons, drugs, alcohol, recalled items, counterfeits, live animals and hazardous materials.
